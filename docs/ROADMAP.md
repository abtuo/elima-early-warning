# Roadmap

## v0.1 (this release)
- Auditable heuristic baseline with explanations and abstention on insufficient data
- REST API, CLI, synthetic examples, tests, container and documentation

## v0.2 (planned, not implemented)
- Configurable observation windows, school calendar handling and data-quality diagnostics
- Standards-compatible CSV ingestion without identifiers
- Multilingual documentation and educator-facing model review templates

## Research phase (subject to governance and funding)
- Pilot partnerships, ethically collected longitudinal datasets and operational definition of dropout
- Study design, stratified evaluation, calibration, fairness and drift monitoring
- Human-in-the-loop intervention workflow, without automated high-stakes decisions
